FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking with Foam Bubble Parties.

What do guests wear to a Foam Bubble Party?
Swimsuits, athletic shorts, t-shirts, or summer clothes are ideal! For night glow parties, neon or white shirts look awesome under our UV blacklights. Bring a towel!
Can Foam Bubble Parties serve schools, daycares, and HOAs?
Yes! We specialize in events for schools, daycares, churches, HOAs, and corporate picnics. We carry $2M commercial liability insurance and background-checked operators.
What setup is required on site?
We just need access to a standard outdoor water spigot and one standard 110V electrical outlet within 50-75 feet of the party area. We bring heavy-duty commercial hoses and extension cords.
What is your weather and rain policy?
We offer a 100% Rainy Day Flexibility Guarantee! If severe storms occur on your date, you can reschedule to any open date with $0 penalty fee.
