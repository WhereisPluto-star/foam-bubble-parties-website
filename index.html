What Is a
Foam Party?

A foam party turns an ordinary backyard, driveway, or event space into an unforgettable celebration. Our professional foam cannon creates a mountain of soft, fluffy foam for kids to run through, dance in, and make memories they’ll talk about long after the party ends. Optional foam colors and fun scents can make the experience feel even more personalized.

  • Kid-friendly, biodegradable foam
  • We bring the foam, music, setup, and fun
  • Optional foam colors and fun scents Blue, pink, green, mango, and bubble gum options planned.
  • You relax while we handle the experience

Big laughs, unforgettable photos, and a party that feels anything but ordinary.
